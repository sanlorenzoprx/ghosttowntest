# Launch Blueprint v2 acceptance fixture

The board-game subscription purchase from July 31, 2026 is the first regression fixture.

The v2 artifact must not pass unless it produces:

1. A founding-family subscription pilot rather than the generic “narrow manual pilot” sentence.
2. A preference profile, curated first selection, use guide, fit review, and founding-member decision.
3. A concrete test price and controlled lower/upper boundaries.
4. A populated Launch Site Starter on Day 1.
5. Ten to twenty-five current, sourced customer-access channels.
6. Five finished community posts.
7. Twelve relationship-specific outreach scripts.
8. Complete landing-page, FAQ, thank-you, and confirmation-email copy.
9. Thirty daily actions that use prepared GhostTown assets.
10. A fail-closed result when research is absent, stale, unattributed, or incomplete.
