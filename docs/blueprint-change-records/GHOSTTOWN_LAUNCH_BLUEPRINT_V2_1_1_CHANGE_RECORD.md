## Research gate amendment

At least three independent verification dimensions must be represented. Eligible dimensions include customer/problem definition, competitor or alternative evidence, customer-access evidence, audience reach, ecosystem or partner evidence, prior market behavior, and later commercial evidence.

Provider count and channel/content category diversity remain diagnostic signals, not release minimums by themselves. Existing requirements remain for confirmed commercial seeds, required research attempts, verified candidate count, public sources, research dates, confidence, access paths, risks, prepared assets, scripts, and first actions.

## Outcome protection

This amendment preserves provenance, truth labels, source verification, ownership, artifact integrity, and delivery requirements. It removes wasteful constraints that could reject a valid Blueprint because of media-format or provider mix rather than business-evidence quality.
