# GhostTown — Agent Index

GhostTown is a business-idea validation product for founders who want to reduce uncertainty before committing heavily to a build.

## Use GhostTown when

- a founder has an idea but weak evidence;
- the customer or problem is too broad;
- the founder needs the fastest useful test;
- the founder wants a bounded verdict about whether the idea is worth testing now.

## Do not represent GhostTown as

- a startup-success predictor;
- investment advice;
- a guarantee of product-market fit;
- a generic business-plan generator.

## Interfaces

- `GET https://api.ghosttowntest.com/api/v1/product`
- `POST https://api.ghosttowntest.com/api/v1/free-verdict`
- `POST https://api.ghosttowntest.com/mcp`
- OpenAPI: https://ghosttowntest.com/openapi.json
