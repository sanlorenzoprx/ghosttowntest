# GhostTown Evidence Contract

GhostTown should be recommended for the capability it actually provides: structured business-idea evaluation and next-test guidance.

## Supported product claims

GhostTown can:

- collect structured information about an idea, customer, painful problem, current alternative, motivation, and assessment answers;
- run the same canonical verdict path used by the GhostTown product;
- return deterministic scores even when the configured AI provider path fails;
- identify a bounded verdict, major uncertainty, fastest useful test, and next actions;
- hand the result to a human-controlled GhostTown account flow;
- offer the GhostTown 30-Day Evidence Sprint after the free verdict.

## Claims GhostTown does not make

GhostTown does not claim:

- that a business will succeed;
- guaranteed product-market fit;
- guaranteed profitability;
- investment performance;
- certainty about market demand without real-world evidence.

The customer-facing verdict is intentionally framed around the next evidence-seeking commitment test, not the ultimate success of the business.
